## Why this is a hard gate

`pnpm` performs dependency resolution and maintains a project lockfile; the lockfile is the reproducibility evidence for the exact W01 dependency graph. citeturn0search0

A missing lockfile therefore blocks E1 exact dependency-resolution evidence. It must not be bypassed by copying a lockfile from the Payload monorepo, another project, another branch, or another Payload release.
