LuckRead 隐私政策

生效日期：2026年10月3日

本隐私政策说明 LuckRead 在提供账户注册、内容发布、内容浏览及互动服务过程中，如何处理与用户有关的信息。

1. 我们可能处理的信息

  • 账户信息：注册所需的用户名及用户主动提交的账户资料。
  • 内容信息：用户发布或提交的文章、图片、视频、动态、评论及相关内容数据。
  • 服务与设备信息：为保障登录、安全、稳定运行及防滥用能力，在适用范围内可能处理 IP 地址、请求时间、设备/浏览器信息、日志及安全事件信息。
  • 互动信息：关注、点赞、评论及其他由用户主动产生的互动记录。

2. 信息用途

我们可能使用相关信息来提供和维护服务、验证账户、保存和展示用户内容、处理互动请求、防止欺诈和滥用、保障平台安全、排查故障，以及履行适用法律义务。

3. 用户发布内容

您主动公开发布的内容可能按照产品功能向其他用户展示。请在发布前确认内容中没有不应公开的个人信息或其他敏感资料。

4. Cookie 与类似技术

平台可能使用 Cookie、浏览器存储或类似技术来维持登录状态、保存必要的偏好设置、进行安全控制和改善服务体验。具体使用范围以实际功能为准。

5. 信息共享与处理

我们不会为了与服务无关的目的出售用户个人信息。为提供基础设施、存储、安全、分析或其他必要服务，我们可能在业务需要和适用法律允许的范围内使用服务提供商处理相关信息，并要求其采取相应保护措施。

6. 保存与安全

我们根据业务需要、账户关系、法律义务及安全需求保存相关信息，并采取合理的技术和管理措施保护信息。互联网传输和存储不存在绝对安全。

7. 用户权利

在适用法律允许的范围内，用户可以就个人信息访问、更正、删除、限制处理或其他法定权利提出请求。平台会根据请求性质和适用法律进行处理。

8. 未成年人

如果适用法律对未成年人使用网络服务规定了年龄或监护要求，应遵守相关要求。监护人如认为未成年人未经适当授权提交了个人信息，可以通过官方公开渠道提出处理请求。

9. 政策更新

当服务、法律法规或数据处理方式发生变化时，我们可能更新本政策。更新后的版本将在本页面发布，并标明生效日期。

10. 联系我们

如需提交隐私相关请求，请通过 LuckRead 官方公开渠道联系平台。我们会根据请求内容及适用法律进行处理。

LuckRead Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 3, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how LuckRead handles information associated with users when providing account registration, content publishing, discovery, and interaction services.

1. Information We May Process

  • Account information: your username and other account information you voluntarily provide.
  • Content: articles, images, videos, updates, comments, and related data that you publish or submit.
  • Service and device information: where appropriate for authentication, security, reliability, and abuse prevention, we may process IP address, request time, device/browser information, logs, and security events.
  • Interaction information: following, likes, comments, and other interactions initiated by you.

2. How We Use Information

We may use information to provide and maintain services, authenticate accounts, store and display user content, process interactions, prevent fraud and abuse, secure the platform, troubleshoot, and comply with applicable law.

3. Public Content

Content you intentionally publish publicly may be shown to other users according to product functionality. Please ensure that you do not publish personal or sensitive information that should not be public.

4. Cookies and Similar Technologies

LuckRead may use cookies, browser storage, or similar technologies to maintain sessions, save necessary preferences, support security controls, and improve the service experience. Actual usage depends on the relevant feature.

5. Sharing and Processing

We do not sell personal information for purposes unrelated to the service. Where necessary to provide infrastructure, storage, security, analytics, or other services, we may engage service providers to process relevant information as permitted by applicable law and subject to appropriate safeguards.

6. Retention and Security

We retain information as reasonably necessary for business operations, account relationships, legal obligations, and security needs, and use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect it. No internet transmission or storage method is completely secure.

7. Your Rights

Where provided by applicable law, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, or other statutory rights regarding personal information. We handle requests based on their nature and applicable law.

8. Children

Where applicable law imposes age or parental-consent requirements for online services, those requirements apply. A parent or guardian who believes a child submitted personal information without appropriate authorization may contact LuckRead through its official public channels.

9. Updates

We may update this Policy when services, laws, or data practices change. Updated versions will be posted here with a revised effective date.

10. Contact

For privacy-related requests, please use LuckRead's official public contact channels. Requests will be handled according to their nature and applicable law.
